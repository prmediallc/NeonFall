## Full description (4000 chars max)
NeonFall is a one-thumb arcade dodger built for quick sessions and long streaks.

You are a neon slider at the bottom of the screen. Red blocks rain down. Gold coins fall with them. Hold the left or right side of the screen to slide, squeeze past the blocks, catch the coins, and survive the stage. Then survive the next one.

STAGES NEVER END
Every stage is a short survival timer. Clear it and the next stage begins with faster, denser blocks. There is no final stage — only the one you haven't reached yet.

SCORING
• Block passes you: +1
• Near miss: +3
• Coin: +10
• Stage cleared: +100

MADE TO FEEL GOOD
• Particle bursts, screen shake and haptic feedback
• Smooth 60 fps rendering on any phone
• Your best score and best stage are saved on your device

NO NONSENSE
• No ads
• No account, no login
• No internet needed, no permissions except vibration
• Under 2 MB
