NeonFall privacy policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

NeonFall is an offline arcade game published by PR Media LLC.

What we collect

Nothing. NeonFall does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. It has no account system, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no internet permission.

Data stored on your device

Your best score, best stage and number of games played are saved locally on your device using Android's app preferences. This data never leaves your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Permissions

VIBRATE — used for haptic feedback when you collect a coin or crash.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/prmediallc/NeonFall/issues.
